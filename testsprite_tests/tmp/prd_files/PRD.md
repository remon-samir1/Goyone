# Product Requirements Document (PRD): Goyone CRM

## Project Overview

Goyone is a comprehensive CRM (Customer Relationship Management) system designed to streamline business operations, lead management, and sales workflows. It provides a centralized platform for tracking interactions, managing deals, and optimizing team productivity.

## Target Audience

- Sales teams and managers
- Account executives
- Business development representatives
- Customer success teams

## Core Features

### 1. Lead Management

- **Add/Edit Leads**: Capture and update lead information efficiently.
- **View Leads**: Access a detailed overview of customer interactions and history.
- **Lead Status tracking**: Monitor the progression of leads through the sales funnel.

### 2. Deal Pipeline

- **Kanban Board**: Visualize and manage deals across different stages.
- **Deal Tracking**: Monitor deal progress, value, and expected closing dates.

### 3. Account Management

- **Company Accounts**: Manage B2B relationships and corporate entities.
- **Individual Accounts**: Track individual clients and personal relationships.

### 4. Communication & Activity Tracking

- **Calls**: Log phone interactions and maintain a call history.
- **Meetings**: Schedule and manage appointments with clients.
- **Activities**: A unified log of all user activities and system events.
- **Calendar**: Integration for managing appointments and time-sensitive tasks.

### 5. Task & Productivity Management

- **Tasks**: Create, assign, and track completion of specific action items.
- **Notifications**: Real-time updates on critical events and reminders.

### 6. Billing & Invoicing

- **Invoices**: Generate, track, and manage client payments and invoices.

### 7. Support & Documentation

- **Help Articles**: A knowledge base for users to find answers and learn about system features.

## Technical Requirements

- **Frontend**: Next.js (App Router), Tailwind CSS, Radix UI.
- **Animations**: GSAP for smooth UI transitions.
- **Rich Text Editing**: Tiptap for creating and editing notes/descriptions.
- **Drag & Drop**: @dnd-kit for pipeline management.
- **API Integration**: Axios for backend communication.

## Future Scope

- Advanced reporting and analytics dashboards.
- Integration with third-party communication tools (e.g., WhatsApp, Email).
- AI-driven lead scoring and automated follow-ups.
